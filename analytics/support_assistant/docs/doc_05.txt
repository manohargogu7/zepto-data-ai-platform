Zepto accepts payments via credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), net banking, and the Zepto Wallet. Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for orders up to INR 2,000 in selected pin codes. COD orders incur a handling fee of INR 10. Zepto Wallet can be topped up using any supported payment method and offers a 2% cashback on every top-up, credited instantly.
